# Staircase: flight to the first floor

The staircase was built against the north party wall. Its two flights lead to the first floor and the attic respectively. [1] The flights have no vault or overhead covering except at the cool store, where the steps themselves form its ceiling. The first flight starts at a landing in the entrance hall. After fourteen steps, badly worn from use and the poor quality of the stone, it ends at a landing giving access to the east room and the main room. There is no handrail. A masonry parapet guards the open side between the landing and the ground-floor ceiling. The stairs are so narrow that there is barely room for one person at a time.
