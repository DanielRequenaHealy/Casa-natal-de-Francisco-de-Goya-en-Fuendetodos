# Room beside the kitchen

This room adjoins the kitchen. It originally opened into the stable and had no direct access to the kitchen. Its exterior door was kept when the Goya Schools were built in 1917 and is still in use. The building of the schools probably led to the doorway to the stable being blocked. Plans to reopen it during the 1982 restoration were not carried out. The room’s original purpose is unknown, but it provided a passage for animals being taken to the stable [see area 03] and was used as a lavatory while the schools were in operation. A small barred window in the east wall looks onto Paseo de Vista Alegre. The ceiling has timber joists with shallow vaulting between them.
